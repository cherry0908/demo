Eastern Sierra · US-395

Fall Color Trip

San Jose → Sonora Pass (CA-108) → Bridgeport → Lee Vining → June Lake → Mammoth Lakes → Bishop → Tioga Pass (CA-120) → San Jose

Oct 11 – Oct 17, 2026 · 7 days, 6 nights. Check Caltrans QuickMap for Tioga Pass status before departure — Sonora Pass is the backup route home.
Two sunrise soaks built in, 10/12 and 10/15. Leave about an hour before sunrise — both sites are primitive, no facilities.
