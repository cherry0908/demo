Eastern Sierra · US-395

Fall Color Trip

San Jose → Sonora Pass (CA-108) → Bridgeport → Lee Vining → June Lake → Mammoth Lakes → Bishop → back north → Jamestown → San Jose

Oct 11 – Oct 18, 2026 · 8 days, 7 nights. Return trip is split across two days via Jamestown instead of one long push home.
Two sunrise soaks built in, 10/12 and 10/15. Leave about an hour before sunrise — both sites are primitive, no facilities.
DAY 1 Sun, Oct 11 San Jose → Bridgeport → Lee Vining
  • 🚗Drive: San Jose → Bridgeport via CA-108 Sonora Pass ~5.5 hrs — start early for daylight over the pass
  • ♨️OptionalBuckeye Hot Springs Off 395, Buckeye Rd is a dirt road for ~3.5 mi, then a short steep descent to the creek — wear real shoes. Upper pools near parking, main pools further down (~135°F, cooler pools downstream).
  • 📷Twin Lakes (Bridgeport) Sunset ~6:26 PM — just minutes from Buckeye, easy roadside access
🏨 Overnight: Lee Vining — night 1 of 3
DAY 2 Mon, Oct 12 Virginia Lakes · Conway Summit · Lundy Canyon
  • ♨️Travertine Hot Springs — sunrise soak Sunrise ~7:02 AM. Jack Sawyer Rd is a rough dirt road, ~1 mi. ~300 yd flat walk from parking to the pools; test temps before soaking, they vary.
  • 🥾Big Virginia Lake to Frog Lakes Trail (Green Creek area) 3.4 mi · 554 ftLast 0.5 mi is one-lane gravel
  • 📷Conway Summit 8,138 ft overlook, quick pullout
  • 🥾Lundy Canyon Trail 0.5 mi at the trailhead, or further into the grove
  • 📷Lundy Lake Tucked into a large aspen grove
🏨 Overnight: Lee Vining — night 2 of 3
DAY 3 Tue, Oct 13 Twenty Lakes Basin · Mono Lake
  • 🥾Twenty Lakes Basin / Saddlebag Lake Loop 8 mi · 915 ftFull day — the longest hike of the trip
  • 📷Mono Lake South Tufa — sunset Close to Lee Vining — tufa towers, one of the most distinctive photo subjects on the corridor
🏨 Overnight: Lee Vining — night 3 of 3
DAY 4 Wed, Oct 14 Parker Lake · June Lake Loop
  • 🥾Parker Lake Trail 3.6 mi · 643 ft
  • 📷June Lake Loop Drive (Silver Lake) 16 mi loop — linger at Silver Lake
  • 🥾OptionalFern Lake Trail 3.3 mi · 1,555 ftSteep throughout; good aspen color + waterfall, but a rough add after Parker Lake and at altitude
  • 🚗Drive to Mammoth Lakes
🏨 Overnight: Mammoth Lakes — night 1 of 2
DAY 5 Thu, Oct 15 McGee Creek · Convict Lake · Hot Creek
  • ♨️OptionalWild Willie's Hot Spring 1-mile dirt road off Benton Crossing Rd, condition varies and can be tricky. Man-made tub + natural creek wading.
  • 🥾McGee Creek to Horsetail Creek Trail 3.6 mi · 705 ft
  • 🥾Convict Lake Loop Trail 2.5 mi · 173 ftColor along looker's-left of the lake and the back boardwalk
  • 🚤OptionalConvict Lake Boat Rental Convict Lake Resort Marina
  • 📷Hot Creek Geologic Site Viewing only — no swimming allowed
  • 🥾Hot Creek Interpretive Trail 1.6 mi · 144 ft
🏨 Overnight: Mammoth Lakes — night 2 of 2
DAY 6 Fri, Oct 16 Rock Creek · Little Lakes Valley
  • 🥾Little Lakes Valley to Gem Lakes 7.3 mi · 1,066 ftRock Creek Road to Mosquito Flat
  • 🥾Rock Creek Lake Loop 1.5 mi · 68 ft
  • 🚗Drive to Bishop
🏨 Overnight: Bishop — night 1 of 1
DAY 7 Sat, Oct 17 Bishop Creek Canyon → Jamestown
  • 📷North Lake (Bishop Creek Canyon) Early morning — best color in the canyon
  • 📷South Lake Dirt roads or the first half-mile of trail into the aspens
  • 📷Lake Sabrina Same canyon as North/South Lake — often the best reflections of the three

Choose one for the afternoon

  • 🥾Option 1Crowley Lake Stone Columns 4.3 mi · 518 ftHike among the columns, on the way north toward Mammoth
  • 🚤Option 2Twin Lakes boat rental Pick this if Twin Lakes still has good color. Drive to Twin Lakes near Bridgeport, rent a boat from Annett's Mono Village, and spend the afternoon on the water.
  • 🥾Option 3Mammoth Lakes Basin trail Pick this if Mammoth Basin still has good color. Duck Pass Trail to Barney Lake 5 mi · 900 ft, passes Skeleton Lake at ~1.5 mi. Or the easier Mammoth Lakes Basin Loop past Lake Mary, Lake Mamie, Lake George, and Horseshoe Lake 5.4 mi · 570 ft.
  • 🚤Option 4Lake Mary boat rental Pick this if Mammoth Basin still has good color and you'd rather be on the water. Rent a boat at Lake Mary Marina in the Mammoth Lakes Basin.
  • 🚗Drive: Lee Vining → Jamestown via CA-108 Sonora Pass ~3 hrs, with stops along the way
🏨 Overnight: Jamestown — night 1 of 1
DAY 8 Sun, Oct 18 Jamestown → Home
  • 🚗Drive: Jamestown → San Jose ~2.5–3 hrs, easy Sunday morning drive
