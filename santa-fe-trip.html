The middle four days swap freely. Santa Fe, Los Alamos + Bandelier, Abiquiú, and Taos don't depend on each other — book the O'Keeffe Home & Studio tour first for whichever day has a slot (Mon or Tue), then arrange the rest around it. Reorder if anything's closed that day.

FRI 11/20

Arrive

  • Land ABQ 3:00 PM, pick up rental car, drive to Santa Fe (~1 hr)
  • Check in, evening on the Plaza, dinner
SAT 11/21

Santa Fe

flexible
  • Georgia O'Keeffe Museum (downtown)
  • Canyon Road galleries
  • Palace of the Governors, Cathedral Basilica
  • Loretto Chapel — the "miraculous staircase"
  • Inn & Spa at Loretto — stop for photos, next door to the chapel
  • Cross of the Martyrs at sunset
SUN 11/22

Los Alamos + Bandelier

flexible
  • Bradbury Science Museum — Manhattan Project history, free
  • Fuller Lodge / Los Alamos History Museum, Oppenheimer's house (exterior only)
  • Bandelier National Monument (~15 min away) — Main Loop Trail, Alcove House ladder climb if time allows
MON 11/23

Abiquiú

flexible · or Tue
  • O'Keeffe Home & Studio guided tour — book in advance, reservations essentially mandatory
  • Ghost Ranch, Rio Chama valley views
  • Bode's General Store
  • Doesn't take the full day — back in Santa Fe by early-to-mid afternoon, with time for New Mexico Museum of Art (optional) and New Mexico History Museum (optional)
TUE 11/24

Taos

flexible · or Mon
  • Via the High Road — depart Santa Fe ~8:00 AM · Chimayó: El Santuario, weaving shops
  • Las Trampas, Truchas — mountain villages, views
  • Taos Plaza
  • Taos Pueblo — no reservation needed, $25/adult at the gate; call ahead to confirm no ceremonial closure
  • St. Francis of Assisi Mission Church
  • Millicent Rogers Museum
  • Rio Grande Gorge Bridge
  • Return via the Low Road, following the Rio Grande (~1.5 hrs)
WED 11/25

Albuquerque & fly home

  • Check out, drive to ABQ (~1 hr)
  • Breaking Bad spots: Walter White's house (private home — view respectfully from outside), Twisters/Los Pollos Hermanos, the Dog House, A1 Car Wash
  • Old Town Albuquerque, Sandia Peak Tramway if time allows
  • Return rental car, depart ABQ 7:19 PM
